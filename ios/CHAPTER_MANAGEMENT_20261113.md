# Chapter management and dense-chart rendering (2026-11-13)

- Bulk song selection includes Move to Chapter, with one library commit for the whole selection.
- Each custom/official chapter has Manage Chapter Songs. Search the chapter list or all official songs, multi-select, then add references or move. Official audio/charts/save identity remain native; copied native chapter entries retain their unlock requirements. Moving back to an original chapter is supported. Deleting a custom destination restores moved official songs to their native chapters.
- Song info buttons provide cover replacement and title/composer edits. Official chapter covers and metadata can also be edited. Official cover storage is separate from custom-chart detection, so official levels do not become custom levels.
- Expanded chapter covers follow Android crop_layout.inc: center the source sprite in the expanded1085x800 window, preserve aspect, let the collapsed polygon mask clip a strip. Applies to the transition image as well. Blurred background sizing is unchanged.
- Chapter export keeps Android v3 manifest fields, writes compact chapter.json, supplies previewStart/difficultyText aliases, names the artifact .phi-chapter.zip and puts root manifest first. Single-song exports remain .zip.
- Both supplied archives already have complete root songs arrays and correct SHA-256/size inventories (iOS11 songs, Android12). The reported Android missing-manifest error was NOT reproduced from these files. Need retest on the actual Android importer; these format/layout changes do not prove the reported error fixed.
